# Phase 11 - Candidate Expertise & Hiring Reputation

The Consumer candidate swipe card and candidate detail screen now show decision-support data before accepting a Match:

- Provider-only average rating;
- received Provider-role rating count;
- successful connection count;
- top 3 job categories from successful Provider connections.

Candidate identity remains masked before acceptance. Reputation and expertise are not masked because their purpose is to help the Consumer decide whether the applicant fits the job.

Candidates without history are presented as new members instead of receiving fabricated scores.

No Cloudinary or Notification configuration is introduced in this phase.
